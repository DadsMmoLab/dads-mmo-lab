VERDICT
  There is no Windows shortfall. 3,913 is the correct, complete count for a
  case-insensitive filesystem; the 1,163 "missing" entries are duplicate byte-identical
  copies of files that are present under their other spelling. The direction of the
  defect is the reverse of the premise: the LINUX extraction is the deficient one, missing
  74,706 WMO-interior doodad placements (+17.4% on Windows) because of the upstream
  case bug above.

  Ours to fix: nothing on the Windows side. Do NOT "fix" the count.
  Upstream (CMaNGOS vmap_extractor): ExtractSingleModel() should apply fixnamen()/
  fixname2() to fixedName before writing the file, so the write and the later fopen()
  agree; and Doodad::ExtractSet()'s `continue` should at minimum count and report the
  drops instead of failing silently.
  Ours to fix if we do not carry a patch: a Buildings/ count is not a valid cross-platform
  equality check. Compare case-insensitively, or compare dir_bin/vmaps instead.
