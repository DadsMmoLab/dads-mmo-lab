Two things fall out of the pair that neither file settles alone:

1. The clause's '127.0.0.1:8085' is a statement about the moment of capture, not
   about the install. This build's install ALREADY advertises the LAN address by
   the time it says ready -- press3.log line 3326: 'The realm now advertises
   172.30.55.119, so players on other machines can reach this server'. The auth
   log still says 127.0.0.1 there because the authserver reads the realmlist at
   startup and had started before that write. So a gate that captures the auth
   log at ready gets 127.0.0.1:8085, and one that captures it after a restart
   gets the LAN address; both are correct and the clause needs to say which.

2. The realm-address change reaches the client only after the authserver is
   restarted, which is exactly what networking.apply() reported:
   restart_required = True (account-lan.log). The pair is the receipt for that
   flag: same server, same realmlist row, two different advertised addresses,
   the restart in between.
