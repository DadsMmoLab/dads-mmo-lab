# Yu'lon user guide

Yu'lon is an app with buttons that installs and runs a private World of Warcraft server (and a few other game servers) on your own computer. This guide is for players and parents who want a server of their own without learning Docker or the command line. You bring your own game client; Yu'lon never hands one out.

- [Getting started](/getting-started/index.md) - download the app, install your first server, keep Yu'lon up to date and use a controller.
- [Running a server](/running-a-server/index.md) - start and stop, back up, update, add modules, play with friends and fix problems.
- [Games and bots](/games-and-bots/index.md) - the games in the Catalog, the playerbots that fill your world, and My Party.
- [Playing](/playing/index.md) - point Yu'lon at your client, make a ready-to-play copy, use the Play button, add-ons and patches.
