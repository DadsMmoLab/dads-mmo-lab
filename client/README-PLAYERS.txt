WRATH UNBOUND — Client Add-Ons
==============================
Cross-class talents, multi-resource bars, and an uncapped spellbook for
Dad's MMO Lab WotLK "Wrath Unbound" servers.

WHAT'S NEW IN THIS PACK (2026-07-25)
------------------------------------
- Respec now clears EVERY talent — your own class tree as well as every
  cross-class tree — and refunds all of the points at once.
- The Mentor sells talent points for 75 gold each. Bought points are yours
  permanently: they survive logging out, levelling up and respeccing.
- The Unbound Spellbook was missing abilities that come from class quests
  rather than from a trainer. Fixed for every class — warlock demons
  (Voidwalker, Succubus, Felhunter, Infernal, Doomguard), the hunter pet
  commands (Call Pet, Revive Pet, Dismiss Pet, Feed Pet), Death Gate and
  Runeforging, warrior Taunt and Sunder Armor, druid Growl, and more.

INSTALL (or UPDATE — just overwrite, your saved data is untouched)
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Fully close World of Warcraft.
2. Extract this zip into your client's AddOns folder so you end up with:
      World of Warcraft/Interface/AddOns/multiclass-talents-ui/
      World of Warcraft/Interface/AddOns/multiclass-resources/
      World of Warcraft/Interface/AddOns/UnboundSpellbook/
3. Launch WoW. At the character screen, click "AddOns" (bottom-left) and make
   sure all three are enabled and "Load out of date AddOns" is checked.
4. Log in.

USING IT
--------
- Talents button (or /mc): opens the Unbound talent panel. Pick talents in any
  class you've UNLOCKED at the Mentor. Click to stage, then Confirm to apply.
  Points are shared with your own class's talents, and Respec hands back
  everything you have spent, in every tree.
- Buying talent points: talk to the Mentor, choose "Buy talent points", and
  pick 1, 5 or 10 at 75g each.
- Spellbook button: opens the uncapped Unbound Spellbook (Shift-click it for
  Blizzard's original — pet book, professions, skills live there). Also /usbk.
  If something you just learned is missing, /usbkrescan forces a re-scan.
- /mcr : options for the multi-resource bar (lock/unlock, scale).

Updating later: close WoW, extract a newer zip over the old folders, done.
Nothing you configure or learn is stored in these folders, so overwriting is safe.
